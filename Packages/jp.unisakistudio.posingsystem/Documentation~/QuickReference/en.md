## Features

### Mirror

Add a menu toggle to mirror your pose.

### Disable Gestures

Add a toggle to lock finger gestures, even when gripping the controller.

### Breathing

Toggle breathing in VRChat. Other users can see the change.

### Roll Over

Roll over by tilting your head. Supported by certain lying poses.

### Foot Height

Adjust foot height while keeping your head in place to correct floating or sunken feet.

### Floor Height

Move your whole body, including your head, up or down by up to 3 m. Floor Height is neutral at 50%.

### Photo Mode

Add Photo Mode to move up to 3 m forward and rotate for a better view of yourself.

### Switch lying poses by jumping

When both supine and prone poses are selected, jump in place to switch between them.

### Reduce pose distortion from head rotation

Reduce distortion when your head faces a different direction from the original pose with head lock off.

### Sync Photo Mode

Share Photo Mode position and rotation with others. Local-only photography uses 0 bits.

### Keep your head visible in Photo Mode

Keep your head visible locally for close-up photos. Normal visibility returns after Photo Mode.

### Disable VRChat idle sway

Disable VRChat's default idle sway while keeping this tool's breathing animation.

### Animate breathing

Animate the chest with breathing. Set the strength per pose without extra sync bits.

### Generate pose icons

Use thumbnails to identify poses. Avatar builds take a little longer.

### Reduce icon size

Reduce avatar size at the cost of some icon detail.

### Enable avatar correction

Automatically correct differences in joint direction and twist for each avatar.

### Prevent reverse knee bending

Prevent the knees from bending backward when the legs straighten.

### Use original standing, crouching and prone poses

Detect motions from the avatar's Base layer during generation. They are not added to the import list. Manual selections take priority.

### Integrate tracking locks with other tools

Enable this if tracking locks do not work correctly alongside other tools.

## Convenience

### Check settings

Select the affected object under Check settings in the Inspector, correct the listed issues, and try again.

### Update prebuild

Generate settings in advance to shorten avatar builds.

### Apply this preset

Choose a starting pose set. This replaces the current pose settings.

### Animation Adjustment

Preview and fine-tune your poses.

### Retake icons

Use this after changing the outfit or appearance. Update the prebuild after changing settings.

### Clear manual selection

Clears manual settings and adjustments for this motion type. If automatic import is enabled, the next build detects it again. This can be undone.
